# Facilitator guide

This guide supports a bounded 90-minute team discussion using the review kit. It is a proposed workshop format, not a record of a delivered session.

## Before the session

- Send participants the `README.md` and `WORKSHEET.md`.
- Ask each participant to record an individual decision before the group discussion.
- Confirm that the example is illustrative and contains no production or employer data.
- Prepare a shared copy of the worksheet and identify one person to capture the team's output.

## Suggested run of show

| Time | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Frame the decision and the limits of the exercise | Shared understanding of the destination requirement |
| 10–25 min | Individual review of the proposed mapper | One decision and the evidence behind it per participant |
| 25–45 min | Compare decisions and locate disagreements | A list of assumptions, missing cases, and policy questions |
| 45–60 min | Inspect the worked answer and runnable example | A distinction between observed code behaviour and interpretation |
| 60–75 min | Draft the team output | Decision, owner questions, and one bounded next step |
| 75–85 min | Choose a real review-process experiment | Named owner and observation to collect |
| 85–90 min | Close with limits and follow-up | Agreed artefact owner and follow-up date |

## Facilitation prompts

- Which claim comes from the contract, and which one was added by the implementation?
- What does the passing fixture actually establish?
- What would change your decision?
- Who can decide the handling of an incomplete request?
- What can this exercise show, and what would require evidence from a real workflow?

## Boundary

The workshop produces a draft review checklist, ownership questions, and a bounded next step. It does not provide a security audit, production access, certification, implementation work, or a guarantee of engineering outcomes.
